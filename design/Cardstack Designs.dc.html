4cThe opinionated take — write receipts: the audit trail lives in the conversation
Where I'd push: after a confirmed write, the card collapses into a receipt.
Chat is the first UI with a permanent memory — the scrollback IS an audit trail, for free. Today's plan treats stacked stale cards as a problem to patch with refresh affordances. Stale-card anxiety mostly disappears (receipts are honest about being history), reps get "I can prove what I did" without opening the CRM, and every receipt is another quiet brand impression of the signing mechanic. It also gives the mark-removable white-label story a spine: the behavior stays recognizable even without the chip.
