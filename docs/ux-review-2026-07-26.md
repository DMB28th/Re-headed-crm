# Cardstack UX review — 2026-07-26

Hands-on review from a UX-designer seat. Method: drove the **live production stack** end to
end — the deployed MCP server (`crm_home`, `crm_search`, `crm_get_record`, `crm_list_view`,
`crm_flow_start/cancel` against the real Salesforce dev org) and the deployed Studio
(`cardstackstudio-production.up.railway.app`) in a browser: builder loop (add/remove field,
editable toggle, publish modal, versions), Lists, Permissions, Assignment, Home card builder
with live preview, Flows, Custom screens, Connections. Widget interaction internals verified
against `packages/widgets/src`. Design intent cross-checked against `design/README.md`
(ids referenced below). No writes were committed to the CRM and the production draft was
left as found (verified via the publish modal's "No draft changes").

The benchmark the product sets for itself — *"no reason to open Salesforce"* — is the lens
for everything below.

---

## What's genuinely working (keep and protect)

- **The builder core loop is excellent.** Click a field → it lands on the canvas → the REAL
  widget re-renders with LIVE Salesforce data ("Account Type: Customer – Direct") in ~1s,
  with an autosave chip. This is better than Salesforce's own page-layout editor, which has
  no live preview at all. It's the demo moment; protect it.
- **Governance honesty.** `Draft · v4 from v3` chip, "Saved just now", publish modal that
  says *"No draft changes — edit the layout first, or publish anyway to re-stamp the
  revision"* and *"Publishes to 8 users · previous versions are kept — roll back anytime."*
  The 8-user count is real (matches Connections). This is trust-building copy done right.
- **Permissions page opinionation.** "Require confirmation on every write — locked on.
  It's the product's spine, not a setting." Exactly right, both as product stance and copy.
- **Connections trust framing.** "Credentials stored server-side · never sent to widgets",
  scope coverage line, separate admin vs per-user OAuth lanes with clear status.
- **Widget internals are real product, not demo code** (`packages/widgets/src`):
  a proper edit state machine (`ready → editing → confirming → writing → receipt|partial`),
  label-based before/after diff that never relies on color alone, CRM validation messages
  verbatim with per-field ✓/✕ partial-failure and "Edit & retry", re-auth state that keeps
  in-progress edits mounted, host-token theming with dark mode, keyboard-complete
  comboboxes, skeletons with `aria-busy`.
- **Honesty on unfinished surfaces.** Custom screens carries "Preview — not yet live";
  flow cards state the render ladder and fallback. Nothing pretends.

## P0 — trust breakers (small fixes, outsized damage)

These are the things a rep or admin hits in their first session that make the product feel
untrustworthy — the opposite of what a CRM-in-chat needs.

### 1. Recents show raw Salesforce IDs, fake timestamps
`crm_home` → recent items for Tasks render as `00TdL00000DPhVKUA1`, and every item says
"Recently viewed · 0m ago" forever.
Root cause, `packages/crm-adapters/src/salesforce/salesforce-adapter.ts:1245-1257`
(`listRecentRecords`): Salesforce `/recent` returns no `Name` for Tasks/Events (they use
`Subject`), and `name: i.Name ?? i.Id` falls back to the ID; `timestamp: new Date()` is
fabricated at fetch time.
**Fix:** type-aware name resolution (Subject for Task/Event; follow-up query for missing
names), use `LastViewedDate` from the recent API, drop the hardcoded "Recently viewed"
note or make it carry the real relative time.

### 2. Home-card builder contradicts its own preview: "HubSpot" vs "Salesforce"
`/home-card`: the config card header reads **"HubSpot · Dan Ben-Atar"** while the live
preview beside it reads **"Salesforce · Dan Ben-Atar"**, and the trust line under the blocks
says "…HubSpot · via Cardstack". The CRM label in the editor is stale/hardcoded instead of
derived from the active connection. Same family: the Lists page alias placeholder says
"my deals, open deals…" on the **Accounts** page.
**Fix:** single source of truth for `crmLabel` (the connection), object-aware placeholder copy.

### 3. The live preview pane can't be trusted for size or interaction
Three related breaks in the builder + home-card preview:
- The 680px card **clips at the right edge** of the pane (field values, the
  "Salesforce · via Cardstack" chip, and even the explainer text "preview writes are disa…"
  are cut off).
- The **380 toggle visually activates but the rendered card does not change width** — the
  mobile layout is unverifiable from Studio.
- The home-card preview header says **"click tiles & rows to dig in" but neither tiles nor
  rows respond** to clicks (verified on both).
**Fix:** scale-to-fit the preview (transform scale or horizontal scroll), wire the width prop
through, and either implement the dig-in drill or remove the promise.

### 4. A wrong URL slug reads as a CRM outage
`/objects/accounts/layouts` (lowercase) renders **"Couldn't reach the CRM —
CrmObjectNotFoundError: Unknown object: accounts"** with guidance about fixing private-app
credentials and reconnecting. It's a 404 dressed as an incident; an admin following the
advice would "fix" a healthy connection.
**Fix:** distinguish `CrmObjectNotFoundError` from connectivity/auth errors; case-insensitive
slug matching (or redirect); reserve the scary card for actual 401/403/timeouts.

### 5. Home-card task check-off swallows errors
`packages/widgets/src/home-card/card.tsx:144-146`: on a failed `crm_complete_task` the row
silently reverts to open — no message. The record card sets the standard (CRM error
verbatim, "Nothing was written"); the home card must match it, otherwise the product's
central promise (writes are visible and auditable) leaks.

## P1 — daily-loop friction (the "no reason to open Salesforce" gaps)

### 6. Editability is invisible — to reps *and* to admins
- Rep side: at rest, editable and read-only fields render identically; the only affordance
  is the global "Edit fields" button (`record-card/card.tsx:517`, `:663`). A rep can't
  glance and know what they can act on.
- Admin side: toggling a field to **Editable** in the builder produces **zero visible
  change in the live preview** — the one thing the toggle governs can't be seen.
- Worse: the live Account layout has a "Save changes" action while **every field is
  read-only** (`editableFields: []` in the `crm_get_record` payload) — a dead-end edit mode
  reps can enter.
**Fix:** subtle at-rest affordance for editable fields (design 1b's amber-dot family gives
the vocabulary); preview should reflect edit affordances; Studio should warn "update action
on a card with 0 editable fields"; widget should hide Edit when nothing is editable.

### 7. The "add" pickers are raw API dumps
The related-list and action pickers are ~45 unsorted dashed chips: **"+ Assets" three
times, "+ Account Partner" twice** (different relationships, identical labels — clicking is
a lottery), consent/CMS noise ("+ Authorization Form Consents", "+ flow CMS: Notify Content
Author", "+ flow Reset Password"), no search, no grouping, no descriptions. Design 3b/10c
specify the richer pickers (relationship metadata, per-list settings, writes-summary per
flow); until those land, the minimum is: dedupe with relationship names, group
(standard / custom / system), add a filter box, and hide obviously non-rep flows by default.

### 8. Field palette ordering wastes the admin's attention
68 fields in Salesforce API order means the top of the palette is `Account ID`, `Deleted`,
`Master Record`, then nine Billing/Shipping address components. High-signal fields
(Type, Industry, Revenue, Owner, Rating) are buried. The header title/subtitle dropdowns
offer all 68 fields — including `Deleted` and `System Modstamp` — as possible card titles.
**Fix:** rank by signal (custom + commonly-used first), collapse system/address fields into
groups, filter title/subtitle candidates to sensible types. Search exists and works — good.

### 9. No staleness anywhere, and no path to the promised stale-card strip
Confirmed by code sweep: no "as of", no fetch-timestamp display, no stale strip (design 1e
specifies it; CLAUDE.md lists it as open). For a CRM mirror living in a chat scrollback
this is the single biggest trust gap — an old card looks identical to a live one. Interim
cheap win: render `provenance.fetchedAt` ("as of 14:32") in the card footer; the full strip
+ Refresh can follow.

### 10. The metadata-gap CTA leads nowhere
Studio home's primary per-object callout — "**68 of 68 fields lack descriptions → review in
the builder**" — lands in the builder, where descriptions can't be viewed or fixed (they
live in the CRM; the amber dots aren't even explained anywhere). Design 2a has the missing
piece: a "Metadata gaps" nudge card with "Fix in Salesforce ↗" deep link. Until then this
CTA teaches admins that callouts are dead ends. Also missing from 2a: the per-field-chip
popover (API name, type, Required passthrough, permissions cross-link) — clicking a chip
today does nothing.

### 11. Accessibility gaps that undercut a keyboard-first audience
- Studio: the publish modal **ignores Escape** (Cancel works); no focus trap observed.
- Widgets (from code review): no focus management on drill-in/Back view swaps (focus dies
  on unmounted elements); write-state transitions (confirming → receipt/partial) aren't
  announced via `aria-live` (only the error banner is `role="alert"`); combobox lacks
  `aria-activedescendant`.
Design commits to a "full keyboard path" — these are the deltas.

### 12. flow-run is the only widget without a 380px breakpoint
`record-card.css`, `results-table.css`, `home-card.css` all adapt at `max-width: 420px`;
`flow-run.css` has zero media queries. Flows — the longest forms in the product — get the
worst mobile treatment.

### 13. Activity/recents hygiene on the record card
Activity rows render `"x (NotARealStatus)"` verbatim and show a Task's *due date* (Aug 3,
future) as its activity timestamp. Junk in the org is the org's problem, but the frame can
help: relative times, status mapped to the org's labels, due-date styled as "due Aug 3"
not as a timeline timestamp.

## P2 — consistency and polish

14. **Three governance models, one product.** Layouts: draft → publish. Lists: "changes
    apply on the next ask (no publish step)". Flows: "exposing a flow publishes the card
    immediately". Each is stated in copy (good), but the admin carries three mental models.
    Minimum: a shared "live immediately" badge vocabulary on Lists/Flows so the difference
    is visually systematic, not prose.
15. **Zero-count tiles** ("0 My Accounts", "0 Unassigned accounts") take premium home-card
    slots. In "All exposed" mode consider demoting/collapsing empty lists ("2 empty lists"
    row) — or make that the Curated default.
16. **No Audit surface in Studio's nav.** The audit log is the product's receipt spine
    (every write logged), but prod navigation has no way to see it. Even a read-only list
    under SHARED would close the loop admins are promised.
17. **Versions dropdown rows** both read just "Roll back" — label the target ("Roll back to
    v2 · published Jul 24").
18. **`crm_flow_cancel` requires `flowApiName`** although `actionSessionId` uniquely
    identifies the interview — needless chance for the host/model to fumble a cancel.
19. **Confirmation provenance is client-side only.** `requireConfirmation` is enforced by
    widget flow; a direct `crm_update_record` tool call is indistinguishable (to the server
    and audit log) from a widget-confirmed one. Longer-term (M7-adjacent): a server-issued
    confirm token minted at diff-time, so audit entries can carry "confirmed by rep in
    widget" vs "model-initiated". This is the backend half of the signing mechanic the
    design already treats as the product's identity.
20. **Create is not yet in-widget** (`record-card/card.tsx:262` — waits on 10b): "New Task"
    quick action exists, but record creation posts a chat followup instead of an inline
    form. Fine as a milestone, worth tracking as the next rep-visible capability gap.

## The strategic gap (product-level, not a bug)

**The default card is too thin to keep a rep out of Salesforce.** The live Account card
today: two read-only fields, no related lists, an activity feed of junk tasks. Everything
this product does well (confirm diffs, receipts, flows) sits downstream of a card worth
looking at. The design already has the answer — onboarding auto-generation (2c: "read 60
fields → picked 9 high-signal → grouped into 2 sections"), related lists on by default,
and the 15-field split-by-audience nudge. **Auto-generation is the highest-leverage open
item in the backlog**: it determines whether the first rendered card feels like "my CRM,
faster" or like a stub that sends the rep back to the browser tab.

Suggested sequencing (each independently shippable):
1. **Week 1 (P0 sweep):** recents names/timestamps · CRM-label single-sourcing · preview
   scale-to-fit + 380 wiring + remove-or-implement dig-in · slug-404 vs outage split ·
   home-card write-error surfacing.
2. **Next (P1 core):** editability affordance chain (widget at-rest → preview parity →
   Studio zero-editable warning) · picker dedupe/search/grouping · palette ranking ·
   `fetchedAt` footer as the staleness down-payment.
3. **Then:** a11y pass (Esc/focus-trap/aria-live/focus-on-swap) · flow-run breakpoint ·
   activity hygiene — bundled well with the 2c auto-generation milestone since all three
   touch first-impression quality.
