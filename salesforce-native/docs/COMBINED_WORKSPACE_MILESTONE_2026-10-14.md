The drawer can open a full18-character record ID through the existing checked native route to exercise same-frame record switching.

Both actual hosts recovered the discarded reply through the existing Resume action. Independent native proof records exactly one save/audit for each controlled Confirm and zero further audits for Resume, with the same displayed/native receipt. Both hosts also held a real related-page reply, switched records through native Open and released the old reply; the new Case remained intact and no old related rows appeared. Screenshots and private native save/resume receipts are retained by the integration owner.

These results used the explicitly enabled developer QA variant. Ordinary builds default the delivery controls off. They establish controlled SDK-to-renderer delivery recovery and stale-result rejection in the recorded hosts, not natural network-loss behavior, arbitrary transport failures or persistence across full-page reloads. The native writer, authorization, reviewed values, confirmation and audit/receipt execution were unchanged.
